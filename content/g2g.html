Six ideas that carry the whole campaign

These six ideas run the whole way from now to the Sunday after, so every post belongs to something bigger. Pick three if the team is small. The idea bank below has one-off posts to fill the gaps.

01

Former House / Latter House

The theme is a comparison, so the content can be one too. Every post puts a before next to an after.

  • Split-screen reels: the old FOTA Abuja building or first service next to Truth Arena today
  • Members hold up a photo of themselves from their first year at FOTA
  • Hook: “The latter house shall be greater. Here’s the former.”
  • Ask long-standing members and leadership for archive photos this week
02

“My Greater” testimony series

One member a day, 30–45 seconds, answering one question: “Where has God made your latter greater than your former?”

  • Shoot five or six on one Sunday after service, then release one a day
  • Each one also becomes a quote card (two posts from one interview)
  • Tag them so their network sees an invite from someone they know
  • Play a compilation on the screens on Sunday 25th
03

Pure Praise: “Who’s still awake?”

Friday is an all-night starting at 9 PM, so the big story is staying up together. Build the online audience for it.

  • Hourly Stories from 9 PM to dawn, each stamped with the time: “11:47 PM · still going”
  • Pin a comment: “Drop your city — who’s worshipping with us at 3 AM?”
  • End on a sunrise shot over Airport Road
  • Tease it all week: “Bring a jacket. Bring your voice. We’re not going home.”
04

Meet the voices

Nine ministers is nine chances to reach someone else’s audience. Give each one a card and a short clip.

  • Graphic templates: one for the speakers, one for the six ministering in songs
  • Ask each minister for a 10-second selfie invite: “I’ll be at G2G…”
  • Tag them and ask them to share it. Their followers become your audience
  • “Guess the song from the first three notes” game for the music ministers
05

Prayer wall → prayer week

The G2G site already has a prayer wall. Make it a public reason to engage before anyone has decided to attend.

  • “What are you trusting God for in this season?” with a link to the wall
  • Film the intercessors praying over the printed requests (no names showing)
  • Post the running count: “400 requests. Every one prayed over.”
  • Answered prayers become testimonies for next year’s G2G
06

From class to commission

Saturday’s Berean Bible College graduation is full of personal stories. Graduands’ families will share it more than anything else all week.

  • Graduand spotlights: name, what they studied, one line on why
  • “The oldest and youngest in the class” two-shot
  • Printed frame / photo corner at the venue, with the #G2G2026 tag in view
  • Send each graduand their photos quickly. They’ll post them the same night
The runway

From now to Wednesday night

The posting runway, in three phases. This page highlights the phase you’re in today.

Awareness25 → 15 days out

Plant the date and the theme

  • Flyer reveal as a three-slide carousel: theme → dates → the nine ministers
  • Pastor Funke 30-second announcement to camera: what G2G is and who it’s for
  • Launch the “Former House / Latter House” series with one archive split-screen
  • Change WhatsApp profile pictures and banners across church groups to the G2G artwork
  • Record the “My Greater” testimonies now, so the whole series is ready
  • Ask every minister for a selfie invite clip (it takes about a week to come back)
Build14 → 7 days out

Put faces on it

  • Speaker and minister cards, one a day. Tag every one
  • “My Greater” testimonies begin, one a day through to the event
  • Prayer wall push: link in bio, Story sticker, WhatsApp status
  • Street vox pops in Abuja: “What does ‘greater glory’ mean to you?”
  • “How to get there” reel: from town, and from the airport side
  • Sunday announcement clip plus a registration link post the same afternoon
Urgency6 → 1 day out

Countdown and invite

  • Daily countdown graphic. Each day’s number carries one reason to come
  • Minister selfie invites go out, one or two a day
  • “Bring one” WhatsApp status kit: send members a ready image and caption
  • Behind-the-scenes: stage build, lights, sound check, ushers’ briefing
  • Pure Praise teaser: “Friday. 9 PM. All night.”
  • Tuesday night: final trailer plus a “Watch live” post for those outside Abuja

The week after

Don’t stop on Sunday

Most of G2G’s reach comes after the event. The footage from five nights is enough for a month of posts.

RecapMon 26 → Sun 1 Nov

Tell the story of the week

  • “G2G 2026 in 90 seconds” film: one shot from every night, cut to the Friday worship audio
  • Full sermon replays on YouTube, titled by topic rather than by date
  • Graduation photo album on Facebook, and tag the families
  • “The moment I won’t forget” comments thread, then turn the best replies into cards
  • Thank-you posts for the volunteers: ushers, media, sound, welcome team, by name
Long tailNovember

Keep the theme working

  • Weekly “From G2G” sermon clip, with a new hook on each re-post
  • Collect testimonies from the week and post them as “Greater, already”
  • Welcome the first-timers: invite everyone who ticked “first time” to a Sunday
  • Save the best raw footage for next year’s trailer, and label it now while it’s fresh

Ground rules

  • Ask before filming anyone at the altar. Use wide shots or backs of heads if unsure
  • Minors: no faces without a parent’s OK, and never tag a child
  • Testimonies: get a “yes, you can post this” on camera
  • Ministers’ songs: short clips only, always tagged. Full sets stay on the livestream
  • Prayer requests are private. Show paper and hands, never readable text
