Start here

Six ideas that carry the whole campaign

Each of these runs from now until the Sunday after, so every post is part of a bigger story. If the team is small, pick three. The idea bank further down has one-off posts to fill the gaps.

01

Former House / Latter House

The theme compares two houses, so the content can too. Every post shows a before next to an after.

  • Split-screen reels: an old FOTA Abuja photo next to Truth Arena today
  • Members hold up a photo from their first year at FOTA
  • Hook: “The latter house shall be greater. Here’s the former.”
  • Ask long-standing members and leadership for archive photos this week
02

“My Greater” testimonies

One member a day, 30–45 seconds, answering one question: “Where has God made your latter greater than your former?”

  • Film five or six on one Sunday after service, then release one a day
  • Each one also becomes a quote card, so one interview gives two posts
  • Tag each person so their followers see the invite from someone they know
  • Play them all together on the screens on Sunday 25th
03

Pure Praise: golden hour to night

Friday starts at 5 PM prompt, two hours earlier than the teaching nights and still in daylight. The story to tell is: come early, and stay for every minister.

  • “Prompt means prompt”: Stories of people arriving before 5 PM
  • Film the sunset through the doors as the first minister takes the stage
  • A Story for each minister as they come on, stamped with the time
  • All week: “Friday. 5 PM prompt. Come early, come loud.”
04

Meet the voices

Nine ministers means nine chances to reach someone else’s audience. Give each of them a card and a short clip.

  • Two templates: one for the speakers, one for the six ministering in songs
  • Ask each minister for a 10-second selfie invite: “I’ll be at G2G…”
  • Post it as a Collab so it shows on their profile as well as ours
  • “Guess the song in three notes” game for the music ministers
05

Prayer wall → prayer week

The G2G site already has a prayer wall. Use it to get people involved before they’ve decided to come.

  • Ask “What are you trusting God for in this season?” with a link to the wall
  • Film intercessors praying over the printed requests, with no names visible
  • Post the running count: “400 requests. Every one prayed over.”
  • Answered prayers become testimonies for G2G 2027
06

From class to commission

Saturday’s Berean Bible College graduation is full of personal stories. The graduands’ families will share these posts more than any others all week.

  • Graduand spotlights: name, what they studied, and one line on why
  • A photo of the oldest and youngest graduands together
  • A printed frame for photos at the venue, with #G2G2026 on it
  • Send graduands their photos quickly. They’ll post them that night

The week after

Don’t stop on Sunday

Most of G2G’s reach comes after the event. Five nights of footage is enough for a month of posts.

RecapMon 26 Oct → Sun 1 Nov
  • “G2G 2026 in 90 seconds”: one shot from each night, set to Friday’s worship
  • Full sermon replays on YouTube, titled by topic rather than by date
  • Graduation photo album on Facebook, with the families tagged
  • Ask for “the moment I won’t forget” in the comments, then turn the best replies into cards
  • Thank the volunteers by name: ushers, media, sound, welcome team
Long tailNovember
  • A weekly “From G2G” sermon clip, with a new hook each time it’s re-posted
  • Testimonies from the week, posted as “Greater, already”
  • Invite everyone who ticked “first time” on the registration form to a Sunday service
  • Label the best raw footage now for next year’s trailer, while it’s fresh

Ground rules

  • Ask before filming anyone at the altar. If you can’t ask, use wide shots or the backs of heads
  • Minors: no faces without a parent’s OK, and never tag a child
  • Testimonies: get a clear “yes, you can post this” on camera
  • Ministers’ songs: short clips only, always tagged. Full sets stay on the livestream
  • Prayer requests are private. Show the paper and hands, never readable text
